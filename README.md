🧭 About Me
I lead analytics and AI initiatives in an enterprise setting, working at the intersection of data, automation and decision-making.
My focus is on moving AI beyond simple chat experiences into systems that are reliable, governed and genuinely useful:
Systems that reason over structured workflows, not just answer questions
Architectures that combine deterministic logic with generative AI
Agents that select and use tools with least-privilege access
Workflows that keep humans in the loop for decisions that matter
Solutions built with auditability and governance from day one
This profile is my personal lab for learning, prototyping and sharing ideas.
