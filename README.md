## 🧭 About Me

I'm a Senior AI Analytics Manager at **Bunge**, where I lead analytics and AI initiatives in Sustainability. My work has included 15+ data products and data governance practices that help business leaders track carbon, waste and water KPIs across global supply chains.

Before Bunge, I delivered end-to-end AI and analytics solutions at **Unilever**. These included embedding Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) data into product design workflows with R&D and Packaging teams.

Today my focus is on **agentic AI that ships**: systems that are reliable, governed and production-ready, not just impressive demos.

- 🤖 Building agents with **Copilot Studio** and **LangChain**
- ⚙️ Scaling AI responsibly with **MLOps / LLMOps**: version control, automation and CI/CD
- 📊 Telling data stories with **Tableau**, backed by **PySpark** and **BigQuery** for high-volume data
- 🌱 Applying AI to **sustainability**: ESG metrics, GHG Protocol, Science-Based Targets and LCA

## 💡 Principles I Build By

```text
1. Deterministic where it matters, generative where it helps.
2. Route before you reason: not every decision needs an LLM.
3. Extractors report. Code decides.
4. Fail closed, not silent.
5. Every number should be traceable to its source.
6. Humans stay in the loop for decisions that carry risk.
```
